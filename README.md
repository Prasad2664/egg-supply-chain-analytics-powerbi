# Egg Supply Chain Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Power BI project analyzing the egg supply chain, including procurement, supplier performance, transportation, inventory, sales, and market demand. Interactive dashboards and DAX measures provide insights into operational performance and business KPIs.

## Dashboard Pages
- **Supply Chain Overview:** KPIs, revenue trends, costs, and performance alerts.
- **Procurement & Supplier:** Purchase costs, supplier ratings, and lead times.
- **Transportation:** Route costs, transit times, shipment status, and delivery performance.
- **Inventory:** Stock movement, closing stock, inventory turnover, and stock-out rate.
- **Demand & Market:** Sales trends, customer demand, and market price analysis.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## Key Skills
Data cleaning, data modeling, KPI development, time-based analysis, dashboard design, and business insight generation.
